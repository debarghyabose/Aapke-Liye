# Rules for agents working on Member-5 (Entitlement, Policy & Pitch)

- **Domain Ownership**: Member 5 owns policy, disaster entitlement computation (SDRF/NDRF), proof substitution frameworks, legal/privacy compliance (DPDP Act 2023, DM Act 2005), statutory escalation templates (RTI, grievances), government/SDMA dashboard specifications, and pitch/demo assets.
- **Strict Folder Isolation**:
  - **ONLY edit files inside `Member-5/`.**
  - **NEVER modify or touch files inside `Member-1/`, `Member-2/`, `Member-3/`, or `Member-4/`.** Other members' folders belong strictly to them.
  - All rules, templates, schemas, and specifications created by Member 5 live within `Member-5/` (e.g., `Member-5/rules/`, `Member-5/templates/`, `Member-5/docs/`, `Member-5/pitch/`).
- **Memory & Goals Discipline**:
  - Always update `Member-5/memory.md` whenever progress is made, tasks are completed, or the next steps shift. `memory.md` is the living ground truth of what is done and what is left.
  - Any idea, feature request, state adaptation, or enhancement that is NOT being implemented right now MUST be logged into `Member-5/goals.md`. Never drop ideas; park them in `goals.md`.
  - Maintain the architectural blueprint, regulatory foundations, and system specs in `Member-5/prd.md`.
  - Keep `Member-5/README.md` and documentation comprehensive and synchronized.
- **Legal & Policy Rigor**:
  - Relief norms must strictly reflect official Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) SDRF/NDRF guidelines.
  - Data protection must strictly adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023 (consent notices, data minimization, right to correction, India data residency).
  - Escalation templates must strictly cite statutory provisions: RTI Act 2005 (Sec 6(1) and Life & Liberty proviso under Sec 7(1)), Disaster Management Act 2005 (Sec 12 for minimum relief standards, Sec 53/54).
